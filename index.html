Ready to Explore AR?

This HCI web application uses your phone camera to detect standard AR markers and overlay 3D models in real time.

  • 1 Click Start AR Camera below and allow browser camera access when prompted.
  • 2 Point your camera at a printed or screen-displayed AR pattern marker.
  • 3 Use the Rotate and Reset buttons to interact with active 3D models.
